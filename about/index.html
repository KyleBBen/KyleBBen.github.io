Biography

Engineer the impossible into reach.

Kyle Brandon Robin Bennett is a Trinidadian mechatronics engineer, inventor, researcher, writer, and multidisciplinary creator.

Kyle grew up in D’Abadie, Trinidad and Tobago, surrounded by problems that demanded practical imagination: flooding, unstable infrastructure, uneven access to tools, and the everyday necessity of making things work.

That environment shaped a lifelong habit of taking ideas apart, combining disciplines, and building physical answers. He later earned a Bachelor of Science in Mechatronics Engineering Technology from PennWest California, graduating cum laude.

His work now spans robotics, mechanical intelligence, product design, spatial computing, engineering education, and creative writing. The common thread is simple: turn the imaginary into real things.
