Engineering for the future.

Am I buying a physical deck box?

No. The planned products are digital design files. A printer, filament, cards, and any required hardware are not included. No purchases are possible in this preview.

Which printers and sleeves will work?

These are still being validated. The release page will need measured dimensions, an exact sleeve configuration, print settings, and actual test results before compatibility is advertised.

Can I sell what I print?

The proposed merchant option permits one named business to sell physical prints of the specific purchased kit/version. Personal-use options do not. Final terms will be available before sale.

Can I share or resell the files?

No digital redistribution or file resale is included in either proposed license. Merchant rights apply to physical prints only.

Is this a subscription?

No. The proposed offers are one-time purchases for a specific kit/version. Future products, recurring releases, or indefinite support are not promised.

When can I order or join a waitlist?

The product is not available yet. This private preview does not collect emails or payments. The tested files, finalized terms, and verified checkout must be ready before launch.
