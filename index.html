Who is Kyle B. R. Bennett?

Kyle B. R. Bennett is a Trinidadian mechatronics engineer, inventor, researcher, writer, and founder of B3N Industries. His work explores robotics, mechanical intelligence, product development, immersive technology, and the process of turning imaginary ideas into real things.
