Research agenda

Intelligence you can touch.

How much useful behavior can be designed into a machine’s geometry, materials, and mechanisms before software has to intervene?

Mechanical intelligence

Kyle’s central research interest is mechanical intelligence: the use of physical form, compliance, underactuation, passive dynamics, cams, linkages, and material behavior to perform functions that might otherwise demand additional sensing, computation, or control complexity.

The aim is not to reject electronics. It is to assign each part of a system the kind of work it performs best—creating robots that are more affordable, understandable, repairable, and resilient.
