Xylem Water Meter Tester.

A hands-on engineering project spanning control architecture, sensors, PLC/HMI integration, electrical systems, physical testing, and rapid prototyping.

CompletedSensus / XylemEmbedded C/C++PLC & instrumentation

From system plan to working hardware.

This work supported a sensor-driven water-meter test system developed in an industrial R&D environment. The project required translating test requirements into control logic, hardware integration, repeatable validation procedures, and clear engineering documentation.

The resulting work improved metering accuracy by approximately 30% and reduced bench testing time from two hours to about forty minutes.
