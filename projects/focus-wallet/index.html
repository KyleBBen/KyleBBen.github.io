F.O.C.U.S. Wallet

A physical focus system that adds intentional friction between distraction and action.

PrototypeMechatronicsProduct design

The problem

Digital distraction is easy because access is frictionless. Software blockers can help, but they remain inside the same device—and can often be dismissed inside seconds.

F.O.C.U.S. explores a different question: can a small physical mechanism create enough deliberate delay to help someone recover agency before an impulsive action?

The prototype

The current concept combines a 3D-printed enclosure, electronically controlled lock, small lift mechanism, RFID interaction, and planned Bluetooth control. It is being developed as a tangible behavior-design tool rather than a generic smart wallet.

Design principles

Friction, not punishment.

Intentional delay

The mechanism should create a pause long enough for a deliberate choice without becoming hostile to the user.

Physical clarity

The state of the system should be understandable through form, motion, and feedback—not hidden behind an app.

Repairable iteration

The design should evolve through observable tests, modular parts, and documented failure rather than cosmetic polish alone.

Development note: This page documents an active prototype, not a released medical or therapeutic device. Specifications may change during testing.
